# Lyfe · The Simulation

A single-file life simulator. One year at a time. One life at a time. One dynasty at a time.

Double-click `index.html` or open it in any browser. There is no build step. There are no dependencies. Your save lives in `localStorage`; nothing is sent anywhere.

---

## What's in it

- **One year per click.** Press space-bar or the Age Up button to advance.
- **Generational play.** When you die, continue as one of your children and inherit everything they were left — companies, real estate, heirlooms, foundations, secret societies, debts, stigmas.
- **15 countries** with their own tax, salary, life-expectancy, and cultural profiles.
- **100+ careers** across medicine, law, politics, military, athletics, the arts, the sciences, and crime — each with its own event pool.
- **Hobbies that monetize.** Practice a skill to level 60 → side hustle. Level 80 → pro tier. Level 95 → celebrity with crossover offers (acting roles, brand endorsements, biopics) and a fame counter that brings scandals.
- **Companies.** 18 industries from tech to defense to crypto. Each industry has its own deep "power moves" — establish a Mars colony, lobby for war, rush a drug to market, achieve AGI, issue a sovereign stablecoin.
- **Office of Power** unlocks for politician age 35+, or for billionaires ($500B+), or for magical-reality characters. From there: push the nation left or right, broker peace, sign climate treaties, conduct a nuclear test, suspend the constitution, pay off the entire world's sovereign debt, declare yourself Pharaoh.
- **World Ledger** tracks every act forever. Lives saved. Lives ended. Debt outstanding. Pandemic death toll. Outrage level. Memorials and scrutinies that follow the family name across generations.
- **Religion + religious law** — Islamic Shariah, Canon, Halakha, Hindu, Buddhist, Secular Civil all selectable, each with their own marriage / divorce / inheritance rituals.
- **Real estate flipping**, **wedding planning**, **hobby tournaments**, **options trading**, **cult founding**, **black market**, **will & testament with conditions**, **family heirlooms** with inheritance bonuses.

---

## Tech

- One HTML file. ~600KB. Pure HTML + CSS + JS.
- No frameworks, no build, no server, no cookies, no analytics, no fonts loaded from CDNs.
- `localStorage` save under the key `lyfe_save`.

## Deploy

Vercel: drag the folder onto vercel.com/new, click Deploy. Or via CLI: `npm i -g vercel && vercel --prod`.
