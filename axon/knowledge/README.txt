DROP YOUR TEXTBOOKS AND NOTES HERE

Put .txt, .md, .csv, or other plain-text files in this folder. The next time you
launch AXON (with start-axon, so the bridge is running), it absorbs every new or
changed file here into its Knowledge bank and pulls the relevant passages into
its answers automatically. You will see "Absorbed N files" when it does.

- Plain text and Markdown work best. For a PDF or Word doc, either export it to
  .txt first, or use "Feed me material" inside any space (that path reads PDFs).
- Re-launching only re-absorbs files you have added or changed, so it is cheap.
- Organize freely with subfolders and clear filenames; the filename becomes the
  title AXON cites.

This is how you give AXON intermediate-to-advanced grounding in your subjects:
the more real source material it holds, the more accurate and specific it gets,
especially the offline local brain.
