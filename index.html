How to Play
The Ward · by WindowDoom
🏥
The Ward Board
Patients on the left are your active cases. Red cards are critical. Click any patient to open their chart. You can only see each patient once per shift.
📋
No Hints
Action buttons show only the clinical order — no guidance on which is right. Read the H&P, labs, and vitals carefully. Then choose. Then face what happens.
⭐
Scoring
Every decision is scored 0–4 after you commit. HARMFUL ERROR raises legal risk. BEST CHOICE earns full XP and builds your career.
☎️
Consult
Costs −12 Acumen but reduces legal liability from that patient's case by 75%. Use it when you're genuinely uncertain.
⏭️
End Shift
Patients you haven't seen deteriorate overnight. End shift to reset acted patients and admit new ones. Use [E] key as shortcut.
🎓
Teaching Points
Every outcome shows a clinical pearl after your choice. Good or bad — you learn something. That's the point.
70 cases across clinical medicine, surgery, OB/GYN, pediatrics, neurology, psychiatry, and ethics. Keyboard shortcuts: [1–4] pick action · [E] end shift · [Enter] dismiss overlay.
