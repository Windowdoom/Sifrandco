How to Play
The Ward · Director's Edition v3.0
🏥
Ward Board
Patients on the left panel. Red = critical. Click to open their chart. Use the TRIAGE tab to manage incoming patients before they reach the ward.
🔪
Surgery
Surgical cases open an OR entrance sequence. Inside the OR, complete the procedure step-by-step. Suture mini-games require precise clicking. One missed stitch changes the outcome.
💬
Dialogue
Patients speak before major decisions. How you respond affects rapport, autonomy metrics, and legal risk. Listen carefully — patients reveal diagnostic clues in conversation.
🚨
Code Blue
Random cardiac events trigger mid-shift. You have 30 seconds. Each code is unique — VF, asystole, anaphylaxis, tension PTX. Know your ACLS. Know your differentials.
📦
Supplies
Track blood, medications, and equipment in the supply panel. Low supplies degrade care quality. Order restocks carefully — emergency orders cost XP.
📎
Office Day
Every 7 shifts, an office day triggers. Complete charts, approve insurance, and handle peer review. These aren't optional — missed admin work adds legal risk.
